Covered client files: all 40 near/mobile GLBs in this directory. Exact individual
file identities and source hashes are in the accompanying manifest.json.
Preparation history: the original v1 trees introduced original painted cutout
foliage; v2 connected the fuller foliage crowns to branches; v3 added richer
painted textures and revised shapes at the rounded v2 triangle count plus 20%.
Warpkeep's client conversion packed each source model's foliage and bark into
one atlas, mesh and material, remapping UVs for instancing. The static models
retain the source trunk-root pivot and near/mobile geometry. Runtime placement,
LOD choice and visual wind are engine presentation, not source-file animation.

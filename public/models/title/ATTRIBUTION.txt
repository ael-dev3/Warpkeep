Covered client files: warpkeep-title-high.glb and warpkeep-title-compact.glb.
Preparation history: six supplied stone glyphs were arranged into the eight-letter
WARPKEEP assembly, reusing the P and E meshes. High and compact profiles have
reduced geometry, normalized bounds, Meshopt compression and embedded WebP
textures. These client GLBs are byte-identical copies of the optimized release
assemblies; no additional model or texture edits were made for this client.

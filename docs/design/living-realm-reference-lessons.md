# Living Realm reference boundary

The Living Realm work used four public projects as read-only craft references:

- [The Long Silence](https://github.com/achimala/TheLongSilence) — finite audio
  events, intentional silence, deterministic timing, and measured visual review.
- [Starfall](https://github.com/e01-ai/starfall) — narrow subsystem contracts,
  hard presentation budgets, quiet visual areas, and browser-level input proof.
- [Operation Ironhold](https://github.com/StarKnightt/operation-ironhold) —
  layered procedural feedback, trusted browser gestures, and bounded automated
  journeys.
- [Three.js Water Pro](https://www.threejswaterpro.com) — public feature
  categories only, chiefly the distinction between directional shallow rivers
  and broader ocean motion.

The three repositories identify themselves as MIT-licensed. Warpkeep copied no
source, shader, fiction, artwork, audio, preset, or binary asset from them.
Three.js Water Pro was treated as a proprietary public demonstration: its code,
bundle, shader structure, and presets were not inspected or reproduced.

Warpkeep adopted finite procedural cues, voice limits, explicit Water contracts,
full-cell river identity, directional flow, localized banks/foam, and objective
rendered checks. It rejected permanent audio drones, generic hover sounds,
ocean FFT behavior in rivers, a new render loop, unmeasured adaptive resolution,
post-processing expansion, new runtime dependencies, and any gameplay or
authority inferred from the references.

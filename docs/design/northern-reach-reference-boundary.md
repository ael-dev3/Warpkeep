# Northern Reach reference boundary

The Northern Reach presentation studied
[Noniv/snowflow_demo](https://github.com/Noniv/snowflow_demo) at commit
`545039733b74eec742862f161990142c7ca7c7ec` as a read-only craft reference.
That revision is MIT-licensed, copyright 2026 Maksymilian Dendura.

Warpkeep used only general principles: a broad climate field, terrain-aware
retention, restrained fine relief, and bounded quality tiers. No source,
shader, asset, preset, or dependency was copied. The Three.js/WebGL
implementation in Warpkeep was authored independently for its existing
renderer and persistent realm.

Warpkeep did not adopt Babylon.js, WebGPU/WGSL, clipmaps or height textures,
terrain deformation or footprints, PCSS/atmosphere/post stacks, TAA, SSR, DOF,
bloom, raymarching, particles, spells, or surf systems from that reference.
